# Independent project case studies

Mode: Experience / Read. Extends the approved Sky portfolio and the established editorial direction discussed with the user. Build directly from real evidence and recordings; no fabricated concept imagery is needed.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Let the reader experience a design decision, then understand why it mattered. Headlines carry a specific argument; assets are evidence, not filler.
OWN-WORLD: Existing Geist and sky introduction. Story bodies settle into stable project-tinted surfaces: warm parchment for Diary, pale sage for Courage, deep charcoal for Tesseract. Large original product media, open editorial space and compact labelled controls. Shared semantic type and spacing tokens.
STORY: Premise, working demonstration, consequential decisions and iterations, bounded early evidence, reflection, next project. Different interactive centrepiece per project.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Back navigation and live link, large readable title and concise premise, then the wide original product image/video bridging sky and story. The artifact starts in the first viewport.
FORM: Approved sequential editorial story with interactive evidence modules, adopted from the user's two recorded references; normal scrolling, no animation required to read essential text.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
