## Purpose and positioning
Thoufik's product design portfolio, with a focus on interaction design, creative technology and AI experiences. Visitors can understand the work, inspect design decisions, try live independent projects and contact the designer.

## Confirmed scope
Selected work includes ZyephrOS, Airtribe and Aceteroid. Independent projects below it include Tesseract, Tom Riddle's Diary and The Courage Computer, each with a complete case study. NotchPark belongs in Playground as an interactive microinteraction. The sky is the shared portfolio identity.

## Evidence and constraints
User-supplied case-study handoffs and ZIP archives are preserved in work/case-study-evidence. They distinguish historical captures, current saved-version demonstrations and retrospective diagrams. Metrics must retain definitions, date and testing caveats. Tesseract's Axis Lab is a local extension, not deployed Explore. Original diary sketches are unavailable; do not fabricate them. The user directed and iterated these projects using Codex. Contact delivery remains disabled during design. No publishing is authorized.

## Principles
Content leads; motion explains. Ordinary document scrolling. Clear keyboard and mobile access. Preserve real project imagery. Actual audience feedback is not formal usability research.
